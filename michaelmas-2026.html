Michaelmas 2026

Term events from 30 October to 9 December 2026. Times and venues will be confirmed closer to each date — check society channels for updates.

  • 30 Oct

    Welcome Social

    Meet committee and members over tea, coffee, and pastries. A relaxed start to the term for new and returning students.

    Social
  • 7 Nov

    Arabic & Culture Evening

    Informal language practice, Syrian music, and short talks on Oxford–Syria connections.

    Culture
  • 14 Nov

    Society Dinner

    A shared meal at a local restaurant — dress smart-casual and book your place via the committee.

    Dinner
  • 21 Nov

    Syrian Cooking Workshop

    Learn to prepare a classic Syrian dish together in a college kitchen. Ingredients provided; all skill levels welcome.

    Cooking
  • 28 Nov

    Movie Night

    Screening of a Syrian film with discussion afterwards. Popcorn and hot drinks provided.

    Film
  • 5 Dec

    Study & Chat Session

    Quiet study block followed by coffee — a chance to work through reading week and connect before vacation.

    Academic
  • 9 Dec

    End-of-Term Gathering

    Light refreshments and photo board from the term. Help us plan Hilary events over mince pies and tea.

    Social
